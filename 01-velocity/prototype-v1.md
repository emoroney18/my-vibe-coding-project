## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** It was a provider profile page for a home organizer looking for new customers/clients
- **What confused them:** It wasn't confusing to understand but the providers name on the page was too big and it felt a little text heavy
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** Number of bookings for a service provider
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** Aesthetically the provider page can use some work to focus more on the providers work and verification so it is easier for users to discern if this is the right provider for them and book more confidently.
